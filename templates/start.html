For lenders and cooperatives in Nigeria

See how Valo Pay runs loan collections

Valo Pay is software for the team that collects loan repayments. It is designed to keep each customer's consent to be debited, their instalments and their payments in one place, link each confirmed payment to the instalment it covers, and send anything unclear to a review queue. Valo Pay never holds money: each lender connects its own Paystack account.

This demo gives you your own copy of Meridian Finance, a made-up lender, with sample customers, instalments, consents, payments and items waiting for review.

Before you start

  • Every name, loan and payment is sample data. Do not enter real customer details.
  • No bank or Paystack account is connected, so no money can move and no messages are sent.
  • You can act as four team members with different roles. This is not signing in.
  • Your workspace closes after 30 minutes without activity and is then deleted.
  • Credit Desk and Cash Desk are previews of workspaces planned for later. They do not work yet.

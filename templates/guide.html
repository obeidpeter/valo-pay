How Valo Pay works

  1. ConsentThe customer gives the lender permission, at their bank, to debit their account for one loan, up to a maximum each time.
  2. CollectionIn the live service, instalments are collected by direct debit on their due dates. A customer can also pay one instalment with Pay-by-bank.
  3. ConfirmationA payment counts only when Paystack confirms it. It is then linked to its instalment.
  4. ReviewAnything unclear goes to a person. Anything that moves money needs a second person.

Each lender uses its own Paystack account, and Valo Pay never holds money. In this demo, no bank or Paystack account is connected.

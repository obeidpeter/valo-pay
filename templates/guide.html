Start page

How Valo Pay works

  • Consent. The customer authorises the lender, at their bank, to debit their account for a loan, up to a maximum for each debit.Step 4
  • Collection. In the live service, instalments are collected by direct debit on their due dates, or the customer pays one instalment from their bank with Pay-by-bank.Step 5
  • Confirmation. A payment counts only once Paystack confirms it, and it is linked to the instalment it covers.Steps 1 and 2
  • Review. Anything unclear goes to a person, and anything that moves money needs a second person.Steps 3, 6 and 7

Each lender connects its own Paystack account; Valo Pay never holds money. In this demo, no bank or Paystack account is connected.

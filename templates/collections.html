What do the statuses mean?
Upcoming / Due
Not yet collected. No attempt in progress.
In progress
An attempt or payment request is open. Only one at a time per instalment.
Failed
The attempt did not succeed. Retries follow the organisation's retry setting.
Unknown
No reliable result. The instalment is held for review and must not be retried.
Paid
Confirmed by the provider record, not by a browser redirect.

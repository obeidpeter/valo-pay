The problem

Repayments are tracked across spreadsheets, bank alerts and phone calls.

When records are spread out, it is hard to see who is due to pay and which repayments have arrived. Your team has to check bank alerts against loan records. An unclear result can leave staff unsure whether to try again.

  1. 01

    Is the payment confirmed?

    In the planned live service, Valo Pay will check the result with the payment service before showing Confirmed. An email or a return from a bank page is not confirmation.

  2. 02

    What needs checking?

    Unknown is not Failed. Further collection stays on hold until there is a verified result from the bank or payment service.

  3. 03

    Who made the decision?

    Decisions are recorded so staff can review them later. The person who prepares a refund request cannot approve it. Another authorised team member must review it. Approval does not mean money has been returned.

How it works

Two planned ways to collect repayments

The demo shows two planned payment methods using sample records. For live use, each lender would connect its own Paystack account. Valo Pay does not hold money.

Collections

Scheduled payments with the customer's permission

  • Add a repayment schedule from a CSV spreadsheet file
  • Ask for permission to take payments — this is called consent
  • Keep collection on hold for Unknown results until the bank or payment service verifies the result
  • Record why a loan is paused

Pay-by-bank

A payment link towards one instalment

  • Show the amount requested and when the link expires
  • Let customers pay through Paystack using their bank account or a bank transfer
  • Wait for confirmation from the payment service
  • Cancel a request that is still waiting for approval

Who it is for

For teams who manage repayments every day

For repayment teams at Nigerian microfinance lenders, digital lenders and cooperatives. Valo Pay is designed to work alongside your loan system. It does not issue loans or make lending decisions.

Planned later

FAQ

Questions lenders ask

Is Valo Pay available now?

The sample demo is available. Live payments are not available yet. {% include "includes/landing_availability.html" %}

Does Valo Pay hold our money?

No. Valo Pay does not hold money. The planned live service would use each lender's own Paystack account to receive payments. This connection is not available yet.

Which payment methods are planned?

Collections is for scheduled payments with the customer's permission. Pay-by-bank offers a payment link towards one instalment, using a bank account or bank transfer through Paystack. These methods are not live yet.

What happens when a payment result is unclear?

Unknown is not Failed. Further collection stays on hold until there is a verified result from the bank or payment service.

How much does it cost?

Public pricing is not available yet.

Can I try it without talking to anyone?

Yes. Open the sample-data demo page and choose Start the demo. It uses made-up customers and records. No sign-up is needed. Do not enter real customer details.

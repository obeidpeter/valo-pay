Demo guide

A ten-step tour using made-up customers, loans and payments. No real money moves or messages are sent. Follow the steps in order or skip a step.

Before you start

Opening a step switches to the sample person and role shown on its button. It opens a screen; it does not complete the task for you.

The step bar stays with you as you explore. Choose ‘Next step’ or ‘Previous step’ to move through the guide, or ‘End tour’ to keep exploring without it.

  • Preparer: sets up requests.
  • Reviewer: checks another person’s requests.
  • Admin: can prepare and review requests and use Settings, but cannot approve their own request.

Some actions may be unavailable for the sample records you see. Read the reason and continue to another step.

Return to this guide

On desktop, open the name/role Account menu near the bottom of the sidebar and choose ‘Demo guide’. On mobile, open ‘Menu’ first, then the Account menu. If a customer preview opens a new tab, return to the original tab.

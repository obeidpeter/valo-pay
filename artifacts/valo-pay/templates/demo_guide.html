Demo guide

A ten-step tour of a made-up lender. No real money moves and no messages are sent. Follow the steps in order, or skip any step.

Before you start

Each step opens the right page as the right person. It does not do the task for you.

While the tour runs, a box at the top of each page shows the step and what to do. Choose ‘Next step’ to move on, or ‘End tour’ to explore on your own.

  • Preparer: sets up requests.
  • Reviewer: checks another person’s requests.
  • Admin: can prepare and review requests and use Settings, but cannot approve their own request.

Some actions may be unavailable for the sample records you see. Read the reason and continue to another step.
